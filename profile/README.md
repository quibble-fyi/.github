### FYI: it's already an issue.

**quibble** is a feedback button for your web app. They write one sentence. You get an
issue in your own tracker with the page, the browser and the error attached.

- **Nothing leaves your infrastructure.** A note goes from the browser to your app to
  your tracker. There's no vendor in the path.
- **A submission can never create a label.** Tags are checked against the labels your
  tracker already has.
- **No database. No container. Nothing new to run.** Your tracker is the datastore.

See it running in your browser at **[quibble.fyi](https://quibble.fyi)**.
