### The trouble with quibbles

They start small: a button that does nothing, a heading that wraps wrong on a phone. Fuzzy
little problems. Someone mentions one, and the questions start. Which page were you on? Which
browser? Can you send a screenshot? The rest, nobody mentions. They multiply quietly, and one
day the backlog is full of them.

**quibble** gives people a button. They point at what's wrong and write a sentence. You get
an issue in the tracker your team already uses, with the page, the thing they pointed at,
the browser and the error attached.

1. **Add the button.** A developer adds it once, in about ten minutes, or
   [hands it to a coding agent](https://quibble.fyi/install/).
2. **People point and say it.** No account on your tracker, no screenshot to attach.
3. **You fix it.** It arrives with your tags and on your priority scale.

**Three places it works:** [in your browser](https://quibble.fyi/ways/#extension) (an
extension, on any site), [in your app](https://quibble.fyi/ways/#in-your-app) (for everyone
who signs in) and [on your computer](https://quibble.fyi/ways/#serve) (beside an app in any
language).

- **Tested against** GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, Forgejo, Jira and Linear.
- **Nothing passes through our servers,** because there aren't any. A note goes from the
  browser to your app to your tracker.
- **A note can't create a label.** Tags are checked against the ones your tracker already
  has, and priorities stay on your scale.
- **Private fields stay private.** Mark part of a page and its text is never sent;
  screenshots paint it out with a solid fill before the image leaves the browser.
